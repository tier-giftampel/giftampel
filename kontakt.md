## Disclaimer

Tier-Giftampel is a guide only and does not replace veterinary advice.

- The poison list was compiled with care but may be incomplete, outdated or wrong.
- Some product data comes from the free Open Food Facts database. It is entered by volunteers and may be missing or wrong. The information on the packaging always takes precedence.
- Green only means that no known toxic substances were found – not that the product is suitable for your pet. Amount, size, age and health of the animal are not taken into account.
- Suggested vaccination dates are guidelines. The vaccination record and your vet take precedence.
- Plant identification from photos can be wrong. The result is only a hint.
- Hazard spots are users' private notes, not verified warnings. Please report poison baits to the police or the local authorities.

Use at your own risk. Liability for damage resulting from the use of the app is excluded to the extent permitted by law; this does not apply to intent, gross negligence or injury to life, body or health.
